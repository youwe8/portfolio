### Step 2: Organize Your Media (30 minutes)

Create folders on your Desktop with your best content:

```
Yuvaraj/Portfolio-Content/
├── adventures/
│   ├── himachal-trek/
│   ├── skydiving/
│   ├── bungee/
│   ├── paragliding/
│   └── road-trips/
├── photography/
│   ├── landscapes/
│   ├── portraits/
│   └── travel/
├── creative/
│   └── paintings/
└── community/
    ├── game-nights/
    └── social-events/
```

**What to include:**
- **Images:** JPG/PNG, highest quality you have
- **Videos:** MP4, maximum 2-3 minutes each
- **Total:** ~10-30 items (your best work)

**Naming convention:**
- Descriptive names: `himachal-sunset.jpg` not `IMG_1234.jpg`
- No spaces: Use hyphens (`-`) or underscores (`_`)

---

### Step 3: Write Captions (20 minutes)

For each photo/video, prepare:
1. **Title** (short)
2. **Location** (where)
3. **Date** (when)
4. **Story** (1-2 sentences)

**Example:**
```
Title: "Summit at Dawn"
Location: Triund, Himachal Pradesh
Date: March 2025
Story: "After 6 hours of night trekking, reaching the summit just as the sun rose over the Dhauladhar range. This solo journey taught me the power of persistence."
```

Create a simple text file or spreadsheet with this info.

## 💡 Quick Tips

### Photo Tips
- **Resolution:** Keep originals (Cloudinary will optimize)
- **Variety:** Mix action shots, landscapes, close-ups
- **Story:** Each photo should have a story
- **Quality over quantity:** 20 great photos > 50 mediocre ones

### Video Tips
- **Length:** 30 seconds - 2 minutes ideal
- **Orientation:** Landscape preferred (horizontal)
- **Format:** MP4 works best
- **Sound:** Optional but adds impact

### Content Balance
Aim for:
- 40% Adventure/Trekking
- 30% Photography
- 15% Community
- 15% Creative/Painting
